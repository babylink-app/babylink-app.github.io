Alerts

Noise and movement alerts

One alert per cry, with a small photo from the camera.

When a sound lasts long enough, you get one notification for the whole cry, with the last picture from the camera, even on a locked phone. Low reacts only to loud crying, High also to quiet sounds. Movement alerts are off until you turn them on.

Alarms

An alarm only if nobody reacts

Calm by default. If the crying goes on and nobody answers, it rings.

If your baby cries for a while, the sound on your phone is off and nobody reacts, an alarm starts: quietly, then louder. It rings even on silent, as loud as your volume allows. One touch stops it. You choose when: after 30 seconds, 1 or 2 minutes.

Never blind

It tells you when it cannot see

A lost connection, a stopped camera or a frozen picture never go unnoticed.

BabyLink checks all the time that picture and sound really arrive. Wi-Fi gone, camera device locked, picture frozen: you get a notification within about 15 seconds, and an alarm if nobody reacts. It also warns when the camera device's battery is low or it gets too warm.

Your phone

Use your phone as usual

Sound keeps playing when locked; the video floats in picture in picture.

Read, write or watch something else: the video stays in a small window on top, and the sound keeps playing with the screen locked. Alerts arrive as notifications. If BabyLink is closed by mistake, your phone tells you it is not watching any more.

Private

Stays between your devices

No account, no cloud, no server, no internet needed. Nothing is recorded.

Video and sound go straight from one device to the other over your own Wi-Fi, encrypted. Nobody else can watch, not even us: there is no server to send them to. No internet at all? One phone's hotspot is enough.

Family

Up to 4 parents

Add a device with a short code. Everyone gets the video, sound and alerts.

Up to four parent devices watch one camera, each with its own sound and alerts. A new device joins with a short code that works once, for 10 minutes. Any device can rename or remove the others, and two devices can swap camera and parent.

Good to know

What do I need?

Two or more devices with BabyLink on the same Wi-Fi, or on one phone's hotspot.

Does the camera device need to stay open?

Yes. The camera stops when the device is locked, so BabyLink keeps it open and dims its screen to black. Put it on a charger for the night.

Is anything stored or sent online?

No. There is no server and nothing is recorded. Read the Privacy Policy.
